Mountain Trekking Safety Guide 🎒

Preparation helps reduce risks when exploring mountain trails.

💧 1. Carry Water

Bring sufficient drinking water and plan how you will refill safely.

🌦️ 2. Check Conditions

Check local weather and trail warnings before departure. Conditions can change quickly.

🥾 3. Wear Suitable Gear

Use appropriate footwear, layered clothing, and a comfortable backpack.

🗺️ 4. Plan Your Route

Share your plan with someone, follow marked trails, and avoid trekking alone in unfamiliar areas.

⛰️ 5. Respect Altitude

Ascend gradually and watch for symptoms such as headache, nausea, or dizziness. Do not continue ascending if symptoms develop.

🚨 6. Know When to Stop

Confusion, difficulty walking, or breathlessness at rest can be emergencies. Seek immediate medical help and assisted descent where safe.
